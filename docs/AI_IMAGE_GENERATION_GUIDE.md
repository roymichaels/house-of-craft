# Apollo AI Image Generation Guide

## Overview
This guide provides comprehensive specifications for generating consistent, brand-aligned product imagery for Apollo using AI image generation tools (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion).

---

## Brand Visual Identity

### Core Aesthetic
**"Luxury Minimalism + Mythic Discipline"**

- **Mood**: Authoritative, refined, contemplative
- **Style**: Warm cinematic lighting with chiaroscuro depth
- **Reference**: Leica 35mm-50mm photography aesthetic
- **Color Grading**: Film-like with golden highlights and lifted shadows

### Apollo Color Palette

```
Apollo Gold:  #D4AF37  (Primary accents, highlights, metals)
Onyx Black:   #0A0A0A  (Backgrounds, text, shadows)
Ivory:        #E5E0D8  (Surfaces, contrast, breathing space)
Tobacco:      #7A5A3A  (Leather, warm accents)
Olive:        #3E4430  (Fabrics, natural elements)
Steel:        #9C9C9C  (Metals, technical details)
```

### Material Rendering Standards

**Leather**: Show grain texture, natural patina, stitching detail. Warm tobacco and caramel tones. Tactile quality is essential.

**Brass**: Hand-polished finish with natural patina potential. Golden warmth without excessive shine. Show weight and substance.

**Wool**: Emphasize weave texture and drape. Soft but structured. Matte finish with subtle depth.

**Steel/Titanium**: Brushed finish with directional texture. Cool grey tones. Show precision engineering.

**Glass**: Frosted with soft light diffusion. Clean edges. Subtle translucency.

**Stone**: Show natural variation and veining. Polished surfaces with depth. Emphasize weight and permanence.

## Technical Specifications Summary

### Camera Settings (Reference)
- **Focal Lengths**: 35mm (environmental), 50mm (standard), 90mm (detail)
- **Apertures**: f/1.4-2 (isolation), f/2.8-4 (context), f/5.6 (detail)
- **Style**: Leica aesthetic - clean, sharp, subtle rendering

### Lighting Principles
- **Color Temperature**: 2700K-4000K (warm cinematic)
- **Technique**: Chiaroscuro (light and shadow balance)
- **Quality**: Soft yet directional, avoiding flat lighting
- **Sources**: Natural where possible, controlled artificial as needed

### Color Grading Guidelines
- **Highlights**: Warm golden tones
- **Shadows**: Lifted but not flat, preserve detail
- **Mid-tones**: Rich and full
- **Overall**: Film-like quality with subtle grain
- **Contrast**: Strong but not crushing
- **Saturation**: Controlled, leaning warm

### Composition Rules
- **Space**: Generous negative space
- **Balance**: Asymmetric but harmonious
- **Focus**: Selective, guiding the eye
- **Context**: Environmental when appropriate
- **Geometry**: Clean lines and sacred geometry

## AI Tool-Specific Tips

### Midjourney
```
[PROMPT] --ar 3:2 --style raw --stylize 250 --v 6
```
- Use `--style raw` for photographic quality
- `--stylize 250` for subtle artistic interpretation
- `--ar 3:2` or `--ar 4:5` depending on use
- Add `cinematic lighting, Leica photography, film grain` to prompts

### DALL-E 3
```
Professional product photography, [PROMPT], photorealistic, high detail,
cinematic lighting, Leica aesthetic, warm color grading, luxury brand photography
```
- Be specific about lighting and camera angles
- Mention "photorealistic" and "high detail"
- Include brand aesthetic keywords

### Stable Diffusion
```
[PROMPT], professional product photography, cinematic lighting,
Leica 50mm f1.4, chiaroscuro, film grain, luxury brand aesthetic,
highly detailed, 8k, sharp focus
```
- Use detailed technical photography terms
- Include quality boosters: "8k", "highly detailed", "sharp focus"
- Specify lighting technique explicitly

## Prompt Engineering Best Practices

1. **Start Specific**: Begin with product type and key details
2. **Add Technical**: Include camera, lighting, composition specs
3. **Define Mood**: Specify aesthetic and emotional qualities
4. **Material Details**: Call out textures and finishes
5. **Color Direction**: Reference Apollo palette
6. **Quality Terms**: Add professional photography keywords
7. **Refinement**: Iterate based on results
